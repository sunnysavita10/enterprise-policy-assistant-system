# Information Security Policy

## 1. Passwords and MFA
Employees must use multi-factor authentication for company systems where it is available. Passwords must not be shared with colleagues, managers, vendors, or support staff.

## 2. Confidential Data
Confidential information includes customer data, employee personal data, credentials, security keys, unreleased financial information, source code marked confidential, and non-public business strategy. Confidential information must only be accessed for legitimate business purposes.

## 3. Removable Media
Copying confidential company data to personal USB drives or other unapproved removable media is prohibited. Company-approved encrypted removable media may be used only when specifically authorized by Information Security.

## 4. Email and File Sharing
Confidential data must be shared only through approved company systems and only with recipients who are authorized to access it. Public file-sharing links must not be used for confidential material.

## 5. Incident Reporting
Suspected phishing, lost devices, credential exposure, malware, or accidental disclosure of confidential data must be reported to the Security Operations team as soon as possible and no later than one hour after discovery.

## 6. Personal Devices
Personal devices may access company services only where the service and device are approved under the company's device-management program.
