# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines reimbursable business travel expenses for employees of ExampleCorp India.

## 2. Travel Approval
All domestic travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager before booking. International travel requires approval from the business unit head and Finance.

## 3. Air Travel
Employees should book economy class for domestic and international flights. Premium economy may be used for international flights longer than eight hours when approved in advance by the business unit head.

## 4. Hotel Accommodation
For domestic business travel, the standard hotel reimbursement cap is INR 7,500 per night excluding applicable taxes. In Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Hyderabad, the cap is INR 9,000 per night excluding taxes. Amounts above the applicable cap require written pre-approval from the business unit head and Finance.

## 5. Local Transport
Employees may claim reasonable taxi, app-cab, metro, or public transport expenses used for approved business travel. Receipts are required for individual expenses above INR 500.

## 6. Meals
The daily meal reimbursement limit for domestic travel is INR 1,500 per day. Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable.

## 7. Expense Submission
Travel expenses must be submitted in the expense system within 15 calendar days after the trip ends. Claims submitted after 30 days require Finance exception approval.
