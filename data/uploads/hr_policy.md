# Human Resources Policy

## 1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave should be requested through the HR portal and normally approved before the employee begins leave.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate may be requested when sick leave exceeds three consecutive working days.

## 3. Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to two days per week when the role permits and the reporting manager approves the arrangement. Teams may designate mandatory office collaboration days.

## 4. Parental Leave
Eligible employees may take parental leave according to applicable law and the company's current parental leave program. Employees should contact HR for eligibility and duration because statutory and company benefits can depend on employee category and circumstances.

## 5. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to five working days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member.

## 6. Harassment and Respectful Workplace
Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, bullying, or intimidation are prohibited. Employees may report concerns to their manager, HR, or the Ethics Helpline. Retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited.
